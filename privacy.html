FredBot privacy policy

FredBot is used only by its owner, Freddy Rhodes, for his own accounts.

  • What it accesses: the owner's own Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets, Docs, Tasks), only after he signs in and grants permission.
  • Where data goes: it is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google user data: FredBot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: October 2026.
